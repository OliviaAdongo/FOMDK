About Me
"An unstoppable young dreamer with a passion for cars, art, and old-school pop."

Hi, I’m Manasseh Sereka. I was born on September 30th, 2009, and I’m the youngest in my family—proud son to George and Alice Likimani, and little brother to two awesome siblings.

I’ve had Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy since I was 7. Right now, I can’t walk and my hands are starting to get weaker too, but that doesn’t stop me from being me.
I don’t go to school, mostly because of how schools are built and how my condition keeps changing, but that hasn’t slowed down my learning one bit.

I’m really into cars—like really into them. I can talk for hours about engines, different car models, when they were made, and even the history behind car companies. Whether it’s a 1967 Ford Mustang or a 2020 Tesla Model Y, I can break it all down for you.
Drawing is another thing I love—especially sketching cars. It’s like bringing my ideas to life on paper. me to express my creativity without physical limitations.

Music is a big part of me too. I’m into old school pop—Michael Jackson, Prince, and the legends. Their music just speaks to me.
I don’t let my condition define me. I just see it as part of my journey—and trust me, this story’s only just getting started.
